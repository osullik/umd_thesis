\section{Graph Definitions}
\label{section:21-graph-definitions}

This section will introduce key notation and definitions used throughout the thesis. 

The following definitions describe the commonly agreed building blocks and descriptors of a graph in an abstract sense. 
\define{Vertex ($V$)}{Represent the `entities' or `things'. Also called a node.}
\define{Edge ($E$)}{Represent the relationships between vertices. Also called arcs or links.}
\define{Graph ($G$)}{The data structures comprising of the related vertices and edges such that $G=\{V,E\}$. Also called a `network'.}
