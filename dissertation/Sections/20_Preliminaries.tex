\chapter{Preliminaries}
\label{chapter:20-Preliminaries}

This chapter introduces our \gai taxonomy and demonstrates its utility in real-world settings by surveying and mapping \ac{IandS} use cases to the taxonomy.
\gai operates on graphs and runs on systems built on computer hardware, so the remainder of the chapter introduces foundational concepts in graphs, graph processing, and computer architectures that interact to affect the efficiency, effectiveness, and cost of \gai applications.
