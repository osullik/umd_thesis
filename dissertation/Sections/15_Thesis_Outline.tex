\section{Thesis Outline}
\label{section:15-Thesis-Outline}

The remainder of this document begins with a review of related work in evaluating hardware, evaluating graph algorithms and creating benchmarks in Chapter \ref{chapter:20-Related-Work}. 
The main body of the Thesis is broken into two related, but self-contained parts. 
Chapter \ref{chapter:30-Graph-Hardware-Benchmarking} details the problem, research methodology, experiment design and results for developing a benchmark for graph hardware accelerators.
It anchors on the question \textit{`How can we decide when graph hardware acceleration is appropriate?'}
Building on the results of chapter \ref{chapter:30-Graph-Hardware-Benchmarking}, chapter\ref{chapter:40-Community-Detection-Case-Study} uses the benchmark to conduct an empirical investigation of \textit{Community Detection} problems, focusing on answering the question \textit{`Are the typical characteristics of a community detection problem suitable for Graph Hardware Acceleration?'}.
Finally, Chapter \ref{chapter:50-Conclusion} will discuss the results, identify future directions for research and conclude.\\
