\chapter{Introduction}
\label{chapter:10-Introduction}
Graph data structures and the algorithms that operate on them are foundational to computer science.
As the social media platforms of the early $21^{st}$ century have proliferated into the global consciousness, graphs have been increasingly used as powerful abstractions to represent interconnected data collected from the real world. 
Recent years have seen a `Cambrian explosion' of artificial intelligence (AI) applications, and the hardware accelerators used to improve their efficiency~\cite{Regli2022b}.
Tasks that have traditionally been in the domain of graph processing are being challenged by modern, neural approaches to machine learning on graphs.
We term the overlap of graph processing and AI and \gai.

For \gai to be useful in the real world, \nrscomment{say something instead of implementations and environments. what is the combo of those called? systems?} its implementations and the environments they run in must be capable of processing \textit{massive}, \textit{heterogeneous}, \textit{dynamic} graphs under \textit{resource constraints}. \nrscomment{dont need to italicize those}

\nrscomment{replace next 2 with "Maximizing performance given limited resources involves making an informed tradeoff, which requires having a mechanism for evaluating \gai systems under varying conditions."}
As a result, understanding the tradeoffs between the efficiency, effectiveness, and cost of \gai implementations and environments is critical.
Understanding tradeoffs requires a mechanism for comparison. 


Benchmarks are \nrscomment{"proven" reads kind of weird here. replace with "well-accepted"?} proven, effective tools to compare the relative performance of implementations and the environments that they run in. 
Developing a \nrscomment{cut good or replace with a meaningful adj} good, comprehensive benchmark requires a rigorous framework, \nrscomment{"which is the goal of this thesis."} and developing that framework is the focus of this thesis.

The remainder of Chapter~\ref{chapter:10-Introduction} will describe \gai, use a study of \nrscomment{insert "\gai in"}the \textit{Intelligence and Security} (I\&S) domain to illustrate its importance in the real world and provide an overview of the challenges in understanding and improving \gai.
The chapter concludes by formulating the problem \nrscomment{cut straight to "of hot to measure..."} that this thesis studies, namely, how to measure the tradeoffs in effectiveness and efficiency in \gai systems.
