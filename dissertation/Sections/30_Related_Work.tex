\chapter{Related Work}
\label{chapter:30-Related-Work}

An important step in benchmark design, and in empirical study design is to understand the existing work and identify opportunities for reuse, extension and what the gaps are. 
First, a quick review of benchmarking and empirical computer science study methodologies identifies the motivation for benchmarks and broad principles. 
A deeper dive into \gai benchmarks identifies gaps in coverage for dynamic graphs and complicated graph structures like knowledge graphs, and examining prior workload analyses reveals known bottlenecks in \gai processing. 
Finally, a summary of the abstractions, representations, algorithms and accelerators used to exploit and elevate bottlenecks shows how there is still latent performance improvement in current \gai approaches.
