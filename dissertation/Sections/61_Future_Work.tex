\textbf{A Benchmarking Maturity Model.}
Reviewing many benchmark approaches suggests an implicit maturity model in benchmark development. 
For a given field, early benchmarks focus on proving functionality.
Over time, the focus shifts to improving the effectiveness of others' solutions and, finally, to making solutions more efficient. 
Poorly constructed maturity models have gained negative attention recently~\cite{Lahrmann2011, Sadiq2021}. 
So, the formal development of a maturity model for benchmarking was scoped out of this thesis. \nrscomment{this makes it sound like you should have done it but were afraid to. reword it. whats the actual reason they got bad attention? theres obv some downsides to the way they have been done- focus on those as the reason you didnt do it}

\textbf{Constraint-Based Experimentation.}
Real-world \gai systems are required to operate under efficiency, effectiveness, and cost constraints. 
The experiments in section~\ref{section:56-CD-Results} show how efficiency and effectiveness can be easily tracked and limited to simulate constrained environments. 
The \gai benchmark system can be easily extended for exploratory experimentation to investigate how \gai will perform under constraints, making it highly suited for simulating edge-computing, low \ac{SWaP} thresholds, and other business or policy constraints.
