\chapter{Literature Review}
\label{chapter:30-Literature-Review}

In this chapter, we motivate our methodological approach by reviewing benchmark design from an empirical perspective.
First, we discuss the benefits and drawbacks of the two main types of benchmarks.
Then, we review the \gai benchmark landscape to demonstrate critical gaps in the coverage of \gai tasks, datasets, and metrics.
Finally, we survey workload analyses and summarize known \gai processing bottlenecks to emphasize that current benchmark approaches lack support for evaluating improvements on these bottlenecks.
The result of the literature review is our benchmark's design, described in Chapter~\ref{chapter:40-Graph-AI-Benchmarking}.
